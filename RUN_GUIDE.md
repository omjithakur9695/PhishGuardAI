## 2. STEP-BY-STEP LIVE DEMO SCRIPT FOR EXAMINERS

When presenting to your professors and external examiners, showcase the project in this sequence:

### Step 1: Platform Overview (1 Minute Pitch)
- Point to the **Top KPI Banner**:
  - Show the **95.2% Active Model Accuracy** and **0.993 ROC-AUC score**.
  - Mention that inference latency is under **0.001 milliseconds per sample**, meaning this system can sit directly inside an enterprise firewall or gateway proxy without slowing down network traffic.

---

### Step 2: Tab 1 — Deep URL Threat Inspector & Explainable AI (XAI)
Explain: *"Unlike basic classifiers that just say 'Yes' or 'No', PhishGuard AI provides transparent Explainable AI risk factors."*

Click the **Quick Demo Presets** one by one:
1. **Safe Domain**: Click `✓ Safe: Google`
   - **Result**: Score: `10.0/100 (SAFE)`. 0 risk factors. Telemetry shows standard Shannon entropy and legitimate DNS root.
2. **Brand Spoofing**: Click `⚠ Phishing: Brand Spoofing` (`http://paypal-verification-portal.xyz/login.php`)
   - **Result**: Score: `75.0/100 (MALICIOUS)`.
   - **Explain**: *"Look at the XAI attribution cards. The system immediately identified that the URL attempts to impersonate 'PAYPAL' while actually registered on an untrusted '.xyz' domain with unencrypted HTTP on an authentication path!"*
3. **Bare IP Host**: Click `⚠ IP Host Attack` (`http://192.168.1.105/bank-update/login.html`)
   - **Result**: High threat. Explain how attackers use raw IPs to bypass domain registration records.
4. **Algorithmic Randomness (DGA)**: Click `⚠ High DGA Entropy` (`https://xj829zla09qmp-secure.top/auth/session`)
   - **Result**: Show the **Shannon Entropy** calculation (`> 3.8 bits`), proving the domain was generated by a botnet algorithm.

### Step 4: Tab 3 — Quishing (QR Code Phishing) Defense
Explain: *"In 2024–2026, attackers increasingly use QR codes in emails (Quishing) to bypass email perimeter text filters."*

2. The browser matrix decoder decodes the landing URL.
3. Click **"Deep Inspect This Extracted URL"** — notice how it switches directly to the URL scanner and audits the destination!
